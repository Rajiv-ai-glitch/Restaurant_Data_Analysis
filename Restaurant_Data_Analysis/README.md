# 🍽️ Restaurant Data Analysis Using Python

An exploratory data analysis project focused on understanding restaurant trends, customer ratings, cuisine preferences, pricing patterns, and service availability using Python.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores a restaurant dataset containing information about cuisines, ratings, customer votes, locations, price ranges, online delivery, table booking, and other restaurant attributes.

Using Python's data analysis and visualization libraries, the project examines the dataset to identify patterns, compare restaurant characteristics, and uncover relationships between different attributes.

The analysis is organized into three levels, covering **11 tasks**, ranging from fundamental data exploration to more detailed statistical and comparative analysis.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Identify popular cuisines and analyze their distribution.
* Explore restaurant distribution and average ratings across cities.
* Examine restaurant price ranges and their frequency.
* Compare ratings of restaurants with and without online delivery.
* Analyze rating distributions and customer voting patterns.
* Investigate cuisine combinations and their associated ratings.
* Explore the geographical distribution of restaurants.
* Identify restaurant chains and compare their popularity and ratings.
* Analyze rating text and its relationship with restaurant ratings.
* Examine the relationship between customer votes and restaurant ratings.
* Investigate how price ranges relate to online delivery and table booking availability.
* Present findings through appropriate data visualizations.

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses a restaurant dataset containing information about restaurant characteristics, customer ratings, pricing, services, and geographical locations.

### Key Features

| Feature               | Description                                 |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `Restaurant Name`     | Name of the restaurant                      |
| `Cuisines`            | Cuisine types offered by the restaurant     |
| `City`                | City where the restaurant is located        |
| `Aggregate Rating`    | Overall restaurant rating                   |
| `Rating Text`         | Textual category associated with the rating |
| `Votes`               | Number of customer votes received           |
| `Price Range`         | Restaurant price category                   |
| `Has Online Delivery` | Indicates online delivery availability      |
| `Has Table Booking`   | Indicates table booking availability        |
| `Latitude`            | Geographic latitude of the restaurant       |
| `Longitude`           | Geographic longitude of the restaurant      |

**Dataset file:** `Dataset.csv`

The dataset should be placed in the project's root directory.

## 📋 Project Tasks

The project is divided into three levels containing 11 analysis tasks.

### ⚫ Level 1 — Basic Data Analysis

#### Task 1: Top Cuisines

Analyzed cuisine data to identify the most frequently occurring cuisines.

**Analysis includes:**

* Identifying the three most common cuisines.
* Calculating the percentage of restaurants serving each of the top three cuisines.
* Examining cuisine frequency distributions.

#### Task 2: City Analysis

Examined restaurant distribution and ratings across different cities.

**Analysis includes:**

* Identifying the city with the highest number of restaurants.
* Calculating average restaurant ratings by city.
* Identifying the city with the highest average rating.

#### Task 3: Price Range Distribution

Explored how restaurants are distributed across different price categories.

**Analysis includes:**

* Counting restaurants in each price range.
* Calculating the percentage of restaurants in each category.
* Visualizing the distribution of price ranges.

#### Task 4: Online Delivery Analysis

Investigated online delivery availability and its relationship with restaurant ratings.

**Analysis includes:**

* Calculating the percentage of restaurants offering online delivery.
* Comparing average ratings of restaurants with and without online delivery.
* Examining differences in ratings between the two groups.

### 🟡 Level 2 — Intermediate Data Analysis

#### Task 1: Restaurant Ratings

Analyzed restaurant ratings to understand their distribution and customer engagement.

**Analysis includes:**

* Examining the distribution of aggregate ratings.
* Identifying the most common rating range.
* Calculating the average number of votes received by restaurants.

#### Task 2: Cuisine Combinations

Explored combinations of cuisines offered by restaurants.

**Analysis includes:**

* Identifying the most common cuisine combinations.
* Analyzing the frequency of different combinations.
* Comparing average ratings across cuisine combinations.

#### Task 3: Geographic Analysis

Used geographical coordinates to investigate restaurant locations and spatial patterns.

**Analysis includes:**

* Plotting restaurants using latitude and longitude.
* Examining the geographical distribution of restaurants.
* Exploring potential clusters and location-based patterns.

#### Task 4: Restaurant Chains

Analyzed restaurant names to identify chains and compare their characteristics.

**Analysis includes:**

* Identifying restaurant chains based on outlet frequency.
* Counting outlets belonging to different chains.
* Comparing average ratings across chains.
* Examining popularity using customer votes.

### 🔴 Level 3 — Advanced Data Analysis

#### Task 1: Restaurant Reviews and Rating Text

Examined textual rating categories and their relationship with restaurant ratings.

**Analysis includes:**

* Analyzing the frequency of rating text categories.
* Exploring positive and negative rating keywords.
* Examining rating text length.
* Investigating the relationship between text length and aggregate ratings.

#### Task 2: Votes Analysis

Investigated customer votes as an indicator of restaurant engagement and popularity.

**Analysis includes:**

* Identifying restaurants with the highest and lowest vote counts.
* Examining the distribution of votes.
* Calculating the correlation between votes and aggregate ratings.
* Visualizing the relationship between customer votes and ratings.
